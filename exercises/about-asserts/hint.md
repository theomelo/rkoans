# Hint: expectations and reality

`assert` passes when its value is truthy. Try `true` for the first two answers.
For the remaining answers, evaluate `1 + 1` and supply that result.

`assert_equal expected, actual` compares both values and explains a mismatch.
A message on `assert` gives you a clue about what went wrong.
