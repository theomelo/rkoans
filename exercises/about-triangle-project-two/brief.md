# Triangle Project Two

Add validation to `Exercises::AboutTriangleProjectTwo.triangle` in
`about_triangle_project_two.rb`. Raise its nested `TriangleError` if any side
is zero or negative, or if two sides together are no longer than the third.
Retain the three valid classifications from part one. Numeric side lengths
may include fractions. A starting classifier is supplied here so this lesson
works independently of your saved part-one solution.
